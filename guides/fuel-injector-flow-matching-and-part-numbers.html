Fuel injector flow, resistance and part matching

Published 2026-11-20 · 7 min read · English version is the reference

Two injectors can look identical, fit the same rail and deliver different amounts of fuel. Flow rate, resistance and spray pattern are what the engine management assumes — get one wrong and the cylinder runs rich or lean while every sensor reports normal. This guide covers what has to match, and what the codes do and do not prove.

The five things that must match

WhatWhy it mattersHow it is written on the part
Flow rateThe control unit's fuel map assumes a specific cc/min at a specific pressureStamped or laser-marked; often the whole identification, e.g. 0280156094
ResistanceHigh-impedance (about 12–16 Ω) and low-impedance (2–3 Ω) injectors are driven differently — a low-Z injector on a high-Z driver burns the driverMeasure across the two pins
ConnectorUSCAR, EV1, EV6, EV14 and a dozen Japanese and Chinese keys look similar from abovePhotograph it; the keyway matters more than the pin count
Body length and o-ringShort and long bodies, 11 mm and 14 mm o-rings — the wrong one leaks or will not seatMeasure the body, not the box
Spray patternSingle, twin or multi-hole; a different pattern wets the port differentlyVisible on the tip; not interchangeable on GDI

Symptoms of one bad injector

  • A single-cylinder misfire at idle that smooths out as rpm rises — the classic clogged or lazy injector.
  • Rough cold start with a strong fuel smell, then normal running once warm.
  • One plug that is black while the others are the correct colour — swap-test before ordering.
  • A lean code on one bank only (V engines), or long-term fuel trim pulled far in one direction.
  • A dead miss with a fuel smell and a hard start after the engine is switched off: a leaking injector dripping into the port.

What the codes prove

CodeReads asWhat it proves
P0201 – P0208Injector circuit open, cylinder 1 to 8The electrical circuit, not the spray. Check the connector and the resistance first.
P0261 – P0264Injector circuit low, cylinders 1 to 4A short to ground, or a coil that has failed closed
P0300 / P0301Random misfire / misfire cylinder 1Points at the cylinder, not the cause — ignition, compression or fuel
P0171 / P0172System too lean / too rich (bank 1)A bank-level fuel problem: an injector, a leak, or the pressure regulator
P0087Fuel rail pressure too lowCheck supply pressure before blaming the injectors

A clogged injector sets no code at all until the misfire becomes bad enough. If the car has a single-cylinder misfire with a normal ignition system, the injector is the next suspect — and the swap test proves it in twenty minutes.

Test before you buy

  1. Read the misfire counter per cylinder in live data, then swap that injector with a known-good neighbour. If the misfire moves with the injector, you have your answer.
  2. Measure resistance across the pins and compare with the workshop figure; a reading far outside spec is a failed coil.
  3. Use a noid light or a scope on the injector connector while cranking — a pulsing signal means the driver is fine and the injector is the problem.
  4. Check fuel pressure and delivery before condemning injectors; a weak pump produces the same lean codes.
  5. On a high-mileage engine, send the set for cleaning and flow testing first — it is cheaper than eight new injectors and tells you whether the set is still balanced.

Why a set has to be balanced

The control unit trims fuel for the bank as a whole. If one injector flows 8% less than the others, that cylinder runs lean while the trim pulls the whole bank rich — the other three cylinders then run rich, and the plugs tell a confusing story. That is why a partial replacement works only when the new injector matches the flow of the old set, and why serious workshops prefer a matched set.

Finding it in our catalogue

Our fuel injector catalogue lists each injector by OE number with cross-references and fitment. Typical entries: 25335146 for the Wuling Jiabao, Minyi and Happy Messenger 1.1, and 0280156094 for the Bosch-system Great Wall, King Long and Foton applications.

  • Send the number stamped on the injector body, plus a photo of the connector and the tip.
  • Order in sets where the customer is replacing a full rail — state the quantity and the flow requirement.
  • GDI injectors are not interchangeable with port injectors even when the connector matches; say which system the car uses.

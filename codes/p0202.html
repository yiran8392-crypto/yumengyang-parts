P0202 — Injector circuit / open — cylinder 2

OBD-II fault code · Injectors & fuel delivery · updated 2026-11-10 · English version is the reference

P0202 is the same circuit check as P0201, one cylinder along. The difference is usually in the harness route, not in the injector.

What the code actually means

Cylinder 2's injector circuit does not match the expected current profile. Open winding, shorted winding, a broken wire or a driver that does not switch — all produce the same code.

On many engines the injector loom runs from the ECU across the top of the engine and down to the rail. Cylinders in the middle of that run are the ones most often affected by chafe and heat.

What you will notice

  • A hard miss on cylinder 2 at all speeds
  • Rough idle and a noticeable power loss
  • Fuel smell if the injector is stuck open
  • P0302 alongside it in most cases

Our order of checks

CauseOur categoryWhy it is on the list
Failed injectorFuel injectorsThe winding fails. Easy to confirm with a resistance comparison.
Harness chafe at the rail—The loom is clipped to the rail and the clips break with age.
Connector corrosion—Humidity and heat cycle the pins until the contact goes.
ECU driver failure—Rare. Confirm by fitting a known-good injector into the same position.

Test before you buy

  1. Measure resistance across the injector and compare with the same bank.
  2. Fit a noid light to the connector and crank — no flash means the circuit or the driver.
  3. Inspect the loom clips and the insulation where it crosses the head.
  4. Swap the injector to cylinder 3 to see whether the code follows the part.

The expensive mistake

Selling an injector without comparing resistance values. A single out-of-range reading is proof; a guess is not. The comparison takes one minute with a cheap meter.

Questions we get

What is the difference between P0202 and P0302?
P0202 is the electrical circuit. P0302 is the misfire that results. Circuit codes come first when the injector stops firing entirely.
Can I replace one injector?
Yes, if its resistance is out of range and the others match each other. Many workshops replace the set on high-mileage engines for labour reasons.

Refund & Returns

Last updated 30 October 2026 · English version is the reference

Contact us before you ship anything back. Below is what the warranty covers, what it does not, and how a claim is handled.

Overview

We want the part to fit and to work. If something is wrong, contact us first — before you send anything back — so we can confirm the case and give you a return authorization (RMA) number. Parts sent back without an RMA number may be refused or delayed at customs.

What the warranty covers

Our parts carry a warranty of 12 to 24 months depending on the part type. The exact term for your order is written on the quotation and on the invoice, so the same number applies to both sides. The warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear under normal use on the vehicle the part is listed for.

What is not covered

The warranty does not cover: damage from an accident, collision or fire; wrong installation or installation with damaged wiring, connectors or voltage problems; misuse, neglect, water ingress or unauthorized repair and modification; normal wear; a part ordered for the wrong vehicle because the OE number or cross-reference was not checked; and transit damage that is not reported to us with the carrier's record.

How to make a claim

1) Send us the invoice or order number and the part number. 2) Send photos or a short video of the part and, where possible, of the vehicle and the fault code. 3) Tell us the vehicle model, year and engine, and what the part is doing wrong. 4) We review the case — each case is judged on its own evidence, and we may ask for the part to be tested. 5) If the claim is accepted we issue an RMA number and agree with you whether the part is replaced, credited or refunded.

Shipping and timing

Return freight is paid by the buyer unless the fault is ours (wrong part sent, part not as described, or a defect confirmed by us). We confirm who pays in writing before the return ships. Inspection and the replacement or credit are handled case by case; we give you the expected timing in writing with the RMA number rather than promising a fixed number of days here.

Replacements and refunds

Where a claim is accepted we replace the part, issue a credit note against your next order, or refund the invoice value — whichever we agree in writing. Refunds go back through the original payment channel where that is possible.

Contact

Send claims and questions to the WhatsApp number or email address below. Please include the order number so we can find your file quickly.

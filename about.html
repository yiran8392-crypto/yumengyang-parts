About us

Last updated 7 November 2026 · English version is the reference

We buy, brand and ship auto electrical parts for the aftermarket. We are not a factory, and this page does not pretend that we are — it explains what we actually do, so you can judge whether we are the right supplier for your market.

What we do

Guangzhou Yumengyang Auto Parts Co., Ltd has been trading auto electrical parts since 2002. We are headquartered in Baiyun District, Guangzhou, we own two brands — WEILI and STANDOUT — and we supply 23 categories of parts: ignition coils, ABS sensors, fuel pumps, oxygen sensors, throttle bodies, thermostats and more.

Every part we sell is listed on this site with its OE number, cross-references and fitment. That list is the product: if we cannot name the OE number a part replaces, we do not list it.

How the parts are made

We are a trading company with our own brand, and we say so plainly. Parts sold under WEILI are produced for us by long-term manufacturing partners — the same arrangement most aftermarket brands you already buy work with.

What we control is everything around the part: the OE and cross-reference list, the specification, incoming inspection, packing and the warranty. We know which batch came from which line, and if you ask us which factory made a specific SKU, we will tell you.

Where we are

Our office is in Baiyun District, Guangzhou, the trading district the whole Chinese aftermarket buys from. Stock ships within days and export goes out through Guangzhou port — we will confirm the exact port and terms in the quotation.

What we can send you before you order

  • Our business licence, stamped
  • The ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 certificates held by our production partners
  • Real photos of the part, the inner packing and the carton marking
  • The test report for the batch we are quoting
  • Buyers in your region who agreed to be a reference

Ask on WhatsApp and we send what we have the same working day. If we do not have a document, we say so rather than sending something that looks like it.

What we are not

We would rather lose an order than mislead a buyer:

  • We do not run our own stamping or winding plant, and we will not claim to.
  • We are not the cheapest source for a single piece. Our pricing pays off on mixed orders.
  • We do not stock every body panel. Our catalogue is electrics and sensors — that is where our knowledge is.

Why this lookup site exists

The first question every buyer asks is “do you have my number?” — usually with a photo of a sticker on a broken part. Instead of answering that one part at a time, we put 6,819 part numbers online with their OE numbers and cross-references, in five languages, so a mechanic in Lagos or a counter in Dubai can check it themselves at 2 a.m.

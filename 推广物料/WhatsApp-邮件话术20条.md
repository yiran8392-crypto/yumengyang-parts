# WhatsApp / 邮件 / 社媒话术 20 条

> 配套「型号查询站」用。每条都是**中文说明 + 英文正文**，英文可以直接复制粘贴，不用改语法。
> 空白处按方括号替换：
> `[NAME]` 客户名字 · `[MODEL]` 我们的型号（如 WL18-AD001）· `[OE]` OE 号（如 4E0927804）
> `[VEHICLE]` 车型（如 Audi A4）· `[LINK]` 型号页链接（如 `https://你的域名/c/4e0927804.html`）· `[COUNTRY]` 国家
>
> **三条规矩**
> 1. 先给信息，再要订单。第一条消息里带型号页链接或图片，别一上来问"要不要报价"。
> 2. 群发别超过 50 人一批，同一段文字一天发两批就够；被举报会封号，客户名单比一次性群发值钱。
> 3. 不报价格、不承诺库存、不编 MOQ。客户问价就回"我按你的数量算"，把数量问出来再报。

## A. 老客户一对一

### 01 · 老客户很久没下单
**什么时候用**：3 个月以上没动静的老客户，微信/WhatsApp 单独发，不要群发。
**English**
```
Hi [NAME], it's Yiran from Yumengyang Auto Parts. Haven't heard from you in a while — are you still selling the same car brands?

I've added a few new ignition coil and ABS sensor numbers since last time. Tell me which models move fastest for you and I'll send the matching list.
```

### 02 · 补货提醒 + 新品
**什么时候用**：客户上次买过的品类有新增型号时。
**English**
```
Hi [NAME], quick update — we just added [MODEL] ([OE]) to the line. It fits [VEHICLE], same packing and same quality as what you ordered before.

Want me to send the details and a quote for your usual quantity?
```

### 03 · 报了价客户不回
**什么时候用**：报价后 3–5 天没回复，一次就够，别追第三次。
**English**
```
Hi [NAME], just checking on the offer I sent. Was the price too high, or is the quantity the problem? Both can be worked out.

If it helps, I can quote a smaller trial quantity as well.
```

### 04 · 节前 / 旺季备货
**什么时候用**：中东斋月前、非洲雨季前、年底补货季，提前 4–6 周发。
**English**
```
Hi [NAME], production is getting busy before the season. If you plan to restock [MODEL] or any ignition coils, send me your quantity this week and I'll keep the slot for you.

No deposit needed to hold the slot — just tell me what you need.
```

## B. WhatsApp 群发（按国家/区域分组）

> 群发前把客户按国家分好组，同样的产品，说不同的车系。每批 ≤50 人。

```
Hi everyone, Yiran here from Guangzhou Yumengyang Auto Parts.

We produce ignition coils, ABS sensors, spark plug wires and thermostats — mostly for Toyota, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Ford and GM. If you send me an OE number, I'll match it and send the part page with photos and fitment.

```

**什么时候用**：非洲客户价格敏感、爱小批量试单，强调"先小量试"和"混装"。

```
Hi everyone, this is Yiran from Yumengyang Auto Parts in Guangzhou.

We make ignition coils and ABS sensors for Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Hyundai and Chinese brands. Small trial orders are fine — tell me the quantity you can move in one month and I'll quote around it.
```

```
Hola, this is Yiran from Yumengyang Auto Parts, Guangzhou.

Ignition coils, ABS sensors, spark plug wires, thermostats — for Toyota, Nissan, VW, Chevrolet and Fiat. Send me the OE number or the car model, I'll send the exact part with photo and price.
```

## C. 邮件开发信

> 标题别超过 60 个字符，正文别超过 120 词。一封邮件只讲一件事。

### 10 · 首次开发信
**什么时候用**：从 Google / 领英 / 展会名单找到的新客户，第一封。
**English**
```
Subject: OE cross-reference for [VEHICLE] parts — Guangzhou factory

Hi [NAME],

I'm Yiran from Guangzhou Yumengyang Auto Parts. We make ignition coils, ABS sensors, spark plug wires and thermostats in our own factory, sold under our brand WEILI.

If you send me one OE number you buy regularly, I'll send back the cross-reference, a real photo and our price for your quantity. No catalog dump, just the part you actually need.

Example part page: [LINK]

Best regards,
Yiran
Guangzhou Yumengyang Auto Parts Co., Ltd.
WhatsApp: +86 159 1585 9048
```

### 11 · 第二封（换角度，不催单）
**什么时候用**：第一封 3 天没回。给价值，不要重复"请回复"。
**English**
```
Subject: Re: OE cross-reference for [VEHICLE] parts

Hi [NAME],

One more thing that might be useful — we keep a public lookup page where your staff can search by OE number or cross-reference and see the fitment:

[LINK]

If it saves your team time, use it. If you want a price list for the numbers you buy most, tell me which ones.

Yiran
```

### 12 · 第三封（给具体型号，制造"就是他家的货"的感觉）
**什么时候用**：第二封还没回，最后一封。附 2–3 个真实型号。
**English**
```
Subject: 3 numbers we make for [COUNTRY] buyers

Hi [NAME],

These three come up a lot with buyers in [COUNTRY]:

- [MODEL] / OE [OE] — [VEHICLE]
- [MODEL] / OE [OE] — [VEHICLE]
- [MODEL] / OE [OE] — [VEHICLE]

All factory-direct, our own brand. If any of them is on your buying list, reply with the quantity and I'll quote the same day.

Yiran
```

### 13 · 报价后跟进（邮件版）
**什么时候用**：发了 quotation 5 天没动静。
**English**
```
Subject: Re: quotation [MODEL]

Hi [NAME],

Just checking whether the quotation reached you. If the price is above your target, tell me your target and the quantity — I'd rather adjust the offer than lose the business.

If you're comparing suppliers, that's fine too. Send me their number and I'll tell you honestly whether we can match it.

Yiran
```

### 14 · 样品 / 展会邀约
**什么时候用**：客户有兴趣但犹豫；或展会前 2 周约见面。
**English**
```
Subject: Sample of [MODEL] before you decide

Hi [NAME],

If you want to check quality first, we can send a sample of [MODEL] ([OE]) before any bulk order. You test it, then decide.

Also — if you're visiting the Guangzhou / Shanghai auto parts shows this season, tell me your dates and I'll set aside 20 minutes at our booth. Bring your OE list and we'll match it on the spot.

Yiran
```

```
No signup, no price hidden behind a form. Find your number, then message us on WhatsApp.
```

**什么时候用**：配 15 秒产品短视频，文案 3 行以内。

```
[OE] — ignition coil for [VEHICLE] ⚡
Cross-reference + fitment in the link in bio.

#ignitioncoil #abssensor #autoparts #oeparts #carcare #autopartswholesale
```

### 17 · LinkedIn 帖（找经销商 / 批发商）
**什么时候用**：目标客户是 distributor / wholesaler / 修理厂连锁，不是零售。
**English**
```
Most aftermarket suppliers send a 200-page PDF. Buyers don't read PDFs — they search a number.

So we published every part we make as its own page: OE number, cross-reference, fitment, photo, and a direct WhatsApp line to the sales person.

3,500+ SKUs across ignition coils, ABS sensors, spark plug wires, thermostats and spark plugs. If you're sourcing for a distributor or workshop chain, the lookup is here: [LINK]

Happy to run a cross-reference check on your current buying list — send it over.
```

### 18 · FB 汽配群 / 行业群（软帖，不硬广）
**什么时候用**：进当地汽配群后先聊天，别一上来发广告。这条是"帮忙"的口吻。
**English**
```
If anyone is stuck matching an OE number, drop it in the comments and I'll cross-reference it for free — we make ignition coils and ABS sensors in Guangzhou, so I usually have the number.

No need to buy anything. Just save the lookup link for next time: [LINK]
```

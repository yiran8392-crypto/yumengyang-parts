Frequently asked questions 常见问题

Six things buyers ask before the first order. Anything else — send it on WhatsApp, we answer in one working day.

What is your MOQ?
MOQ depends on the part and the packing. Send us the models and quantities you need and we confirm the MOQ line by line.
How long does delivery take?
Stock items leave our Guangzhou warehouse within days. Production orders depend on quantity — we confirm the exact shipping date in the quotation.
What warranty do you give?
12 to 24 months depending on the part type. The exact term is written on the quotation and the invoice, so it is the same for you and for us.
Can I return or exchange a part?
Yes. Send photos of the problem and we replace or credit the faulty parts. Our return rate is 0.03%, so it is rare.
Can I get samples for testing?
Yes. Samples are available on request before a bulk order; the buyer covers the freight.
How do I pay?
Bank transfer (T/T) is standard: 30% deposit, balance before shipment. L/C at sight is accepted on orders above USD 20,000. Terms are confirmed on the proforma invoice.

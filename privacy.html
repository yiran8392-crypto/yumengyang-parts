Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026 · English version is the reference

We keep this short and specific: what we receive, why we keep it, and how to have it removed.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what data we handle when you use our part-number lookup site and when you contact us about a part. It does not cover other companies' sites that we link to.

Information you give us

We only receive what you send us: your name, company and country, the part numbers and quantities you ask about, vehicle details, order information and any photos or documents you attach. You can send this by WhatsApp, email or any other channel you choose.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider keeps standard web-server logs (IP address, browser, page requested, time) to run the site and block abuse. The site also remembers your language choice in your own browser (local storage key ymy_lang); that value stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The language preference is stored in your browser as local storage, not as a cookie, and clearing your browser data removes it.

How we use your information

To answer your inquiry, prepare a quotation, arrange production and shipping, keep the records our accounting and export paperwork require, and to find and fix faults on the site. We do not use your inquiry to build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share data only where it is needed to serve you: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) processes access logs; WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages you choose to send us there; freight forwarders, couriers and customs authorities receive the shipping details required for delivery. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not pass your customer list to other buyers.

How long we keep it

Inquiry and order records are kept for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet legal, tax and export requirements. Marketing messages stop as soon as you ask us to stop.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information you sent, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the email address below and we will act on the request. Deleting an inquiry record may end our ability to quote or support that order.

Security

We keep inquiry data on access-controlled systems and limit it to the staff who deal with your order. No system is perfectly secure, and messages sent over WhatsApp or ordinary email are not end-to-end encrypted by us.

Children

This site is a business-to-business parts catalogue. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we update this page and the date above. The version published here is the one that applies.
